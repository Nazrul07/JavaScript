Debounce & Throttle

Type in the box below and watch the counters

Debounce — waits until the user STOPS acting for a set delay, then fires ONCE. Best for search boxes and form validation.

Throttle — fires at most ONCE per fixed interval, no matter how often the event happens. Best for scroll and mousemove handlers.
